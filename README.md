# T-Radar — Stockholm SL Commuter & Live Widgets

Ultra-lightweight native Android departure board and Home Screen widgets for Stockholm **SL** (Tunnelbana, Buses, Pendeltåg, Roslagsbanan & Trams), paired with the live 3D web radar at **[sthlm-under.lovable.app](https://sthlm-under.lovable.app/)**.

---

## Install on Android (Free, 0 kr)

1. Open the **[Latest Release](https://github.com/dnyx-fd3/sthlm-metro-android/releases/latest)** on your Android phone.
2. Tap **`sthlm-metro-radar.apk`** to download and open it.
3. If Android asks, tap **Settings → Allow from this source**, then tap **Install**.
4. Future updates can be pulled directly inside **T-Radar → ⚙ Settings → Check for updates**.

---

## Add & Customize Your Home Screen Widget

### Fastest Way (Inside the App)
Open **T-Radar → ⚙ Settings** and tap **Pin Board Widget** or **Pin Mini Strip (4×1)** to drop it straight onto your Home Screen.

### Manual Way (Works on Every Launcher)
1. **Long-press** an empty area on your Android Home Screen and tap **Widgets**.
2. Scroll to **T-Radar** and choose:
   * **T-Radar Board** — drag edges to resize seamlessly between **4×1** (mini strip), **4×2** (2 departures), and **4×3** (4 departures + 3-stop tabs).
   * **T-Radar Mini (4×1)** — compact single-row live countdown bar.
3. Configure up to **3 commute stops** (e.g. *Stora Lappis → Universitetet → Odenplan*), walk-buffer reminders, theme (**Auto Wallpaper / Dark / Light**), and **Opacity (0%–100%)**.

## Key Features

* **📍 Near Me + Walk Time:** Finds your closest SL stop offline using GPS and estimates realistic walk time so your reminder rings right when you need to leave.
* **Live Ticking Countdowns:** Counts down locally to the second with zero background polling; once all cached departures leave, a neon cyber-cat appears pointing to `↻`.
* **1-Tap Commute Alarms:** Tap any departure row (in-app or on the widget) to set a labeled timer in your phone's Clock app minus your walk buffer.
* **◎ 3D Live Radar:** Opens the interactive 3D Stockholm Metro map in a fast Chrome Custom Tab without bloating app storage.
* **Wallpaper-Adaptive Styling:** Supports Android 12+ Material You dynamic colors, Light/Dark modes, and 0%–100% widget opacity.
