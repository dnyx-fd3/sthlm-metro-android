# T-Radar — Stockholm SL Commuter & Live Widgets

Ultra-lightweight native Android departure board and Home Screen widgets for Stockholm **SL** (Tunnelbana, Buses, Pendeltåg, Roslagsbanan & Trams), paired with the live 3D web radar at **[sthlm-under.lovable.app](https://sthlm-under.lovable.app/)**.

---

## Install on Android (Free, 0 kr)

1. Open the **[Latest Release](https://github.com/dnyx-fd3/sthlm-metro-android/releases/latest)** on your Android phone.
2. Tap **`sthlm-metro-radar.apk`** to download and open it.
3. If Android asks, tap **Settings → Allow from this source**, then tap **Install**.
4. Future updates can be pulled directly inside **T-Radar → ⚙ Settings → Check for updates**.

---

## Add & Customize Your Home Screen Widget

### Fastest Way (Inside the App)
Open **T-Radar → ⚙ Settings** and tap **+ BOARD (4×2/3)** or **+ MINI (4×1)** to pin it straight onto your Home Screen.

### Manual Way (Works on Every Launcher)
1. **Long-press** an empty area on your Android Home Screen and tap **Widgets**.
2. Scroll to **T-Radar** and pick **T-Radar Board** (resizable between **4×1**, **4×2**, and **4×3**) or **T-Radar Mini (4×1)**.
3. Configure up to **3 commute tabs** — assign any slot to a specific SL stop **or** **`📍 Near me`** for silent, in-widget GPS updates.

---

## Key Features

* **Silent In-Widget `📍 Near me`:** Assign any of your 3 widget tabs to `📍 Near me`. Tapping it finds your closest SL stop and walk time right on your Home Screen without opening the app.
* **1-Tap In-Widget `🔔` Chime Reminders:** Tap any departure row right on the widget to toggle a Doze-proof reminder (`departure − walk buffer`). When it's time to head out, T-Radar plays its custom commuter chime + vibration right on time.
* **Live Ticking Countdowns & Neon Cyber-Cat:** Counts down locally to the second with zero background polling; once cached buses leave, a glowing neon cyber-cat points to `↻`.
* **Wallpaper-Adaptive Styling:** Auto Material You wallpaper colors, forced Dark/Light modes, and 0%–100% widget opacity.
* **◎ 3D Live Radar:** Launches the interactive 3D Stockholm Metro web map in a fast Chrome Custom Tab (0 MB private browser cache).
